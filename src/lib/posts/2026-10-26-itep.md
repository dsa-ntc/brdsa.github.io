We need to talk about the insanity of Louisiana's corporate welfare handouts, better known as the Industrial Tax Exemption Program (ITEP). Set up in 1936, this program claimed that old song and dance of "bringing jobs" and "growing local business" to Louisiana, but of course accountability for those promises was never required. This is perfect for industry as they would be allowed — encouraged even — to [bring their industrial waste with them](https://www.nola.com/news/business/louisiana-gulf-nuclear-waste-storage-plan/article_6c2ae372-82a8-4855-b430-c45a3822a30f.html "https://www.nola.com/news/business/louisiana-gulf-nuclear-waste-storage-plan/article_6c2ae372-82a8-4855-b430-c45a3822a30f.html") (hint: it's radioactive). [Our state rolled out the red carpet further. On top of 100% exemption from property taxes](https://ieefa.org/resources/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program-itep "https://ieefa.org/resources/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program-itep"), Louisiana would relax, better yet, [IGNORE environmental oversight over industry operations](https://earthjustice.org/press/2026/trump-administrations-plan-to-put-louisiana-in-charge-of-toxic-coal-ash-pollution-threatens-sources-of-drinking-water "https://earthjustice.org/press/2026/trump-administrations-plan-to-put-louisiana-in-charge-of-toxic-coal-ash-pollution-threatens-sources-of-drinking-water"). This was so good for industry, they enshrined it in the constitution. No need for those fickle citizens to get crazy ideas in their heads about VOTING on these taxes, or lack thereof. Democracy is so passé. And it gets better - [our state is so economically impoverished](https://www.fcnl.org/updates/2024-09/top-10-poorest-states-us?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23596131997&gbraid=0AAAAAoJx5MkpSniA4mbNEpnMfsgqAZk3s&gclid=CjwKCAjwvsvTBhBaEiwAmf-3ntqU18Vn8I4qWUv_No9JOk425macfgQHwyVFb4gTRfMyz4NiD5RaIBoCdmkQAvD_BwE "https://www.fcnl.org/updates/2024-09/top-10-poorest-states-us?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23596131997&gbraid=0AAAAAoJx5MkpSniA4mbNEpnMfsgqAZk3s&gclid=CjwKCAjwvsvTBhBaEiwAmf-3ntqU18Vn8I4qWUv_No9JOk425macfgQHwyVFb4gTRfMyz4NiD5RaIBoCdmkQAvD_BwE"), it's almost as if it facilitates industry taking our labor for next to nothing. [Forget the certain health impacts tomorrow](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/2025/the-shocking-hazards-of-louisianas-cancer-alley "https://publichealth.jhu.edu/2025/the-shocking-hazards-of-louisianas-cancer-alley"). People gotta eat, today.  
The program owes its success to the [Louisiana Economic Development (LED) Board of Commerce & Industry](https://www.opportunitylouisiana.gov/about-led/advisory-boards/louisiana-board-of-commerce-and-industry "https://www.opportunitylouisiana.gov/about-led/advisory-boards/louisiana-board-of-commerce-and-industry") who decides which exemptions are granted, conveniently[ 99.95%](https://ieefa.org/resources/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program-itep "https://ieefa.org/resources/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program-itep") of them. The process is so streamlined, that in 2015, [600 exemptions were granted in-globo (at once) in one meeting](https://www.opportunitylouisiana.gov/wp-content/uploads/docs/12-8-15_commerce_and_industry_board_meeting_minutes.pdf "https://www.opportunitylouisiana.gov/wp-content/uploads/docs/12-8-15_commerce_and_industry_board_meeting_minutes.pdf"). That would not be the case if the board was required to solicit input from the local areas affected by incoming industry. It's not all good news. For 80 years the tax exemption was 100% with no mandated job creation numbers, but in 2016 then Governor John Bel Edwards changed things up. The tax exemption was decreased to 80%, he mandated a few jobs, and [‘established a requirement that local entities with taxing authority approve any new exemptions.'](https://ieefa.org/resources/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program-itep "https://ieefa.org/resources/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program-itep") But don't fret, once that tree-hugger was out of office, the boot-licker himself, [Jeffy-poo walked all that back with one stroke of the pen](https://ryan.com/about-ryan/news-and-insights/2024/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program/ "https://ryan.com/about-ryan/news-and-insights/2024/louisiana-industrial-tax-exemption-program/"). How is Louisiana going to remain the [‘worst state in America'](https://www.wbrz.com/news/new-study-ranks-louisiana-as-worst-state-in-america-among-10-worst-states-across-multiple-metrics/ "https://www.wbrz.com/news/new-study-ranks-louisiana-as-worst-state-in-america-among-10-worst-states-across-multiple-metrics/") otherwise?

[Just last year](https://lailluminator.com/2025/06/02/red-alert-one/ "https://lailluminator.com/2025/06/02/red-alert-one/"), Atlantic Alumina, known to the locals as ‘Atalco,' was cited 23 times for discharging industrial waste containing arsenic, cadmium, chromium and other toxic heavy metals into public areas and waterways. Water samples tested by the Louisiana Department of Environmental Quality (LDEQ) detected arsenic at a concentration 1,400% above the level considered safe for drinking water.  
In 2025 again, [a chemical fire at the Smitty's Supply facility](https://lailluminator.com/2025/10/02/smittys-supply-neighbors-still-wary/ "https://lailluminator.com/2025/10/02/smittys-supply-neighbors-still-wary/"), which stores petrochemicals, sent smoke, soot, and oily residue into the homes, businesses, elementary schools and 40 miles down the Tangipahoa River to Lake Pontchartrain. [Cameron fisheries have been smothered to make way for the likes of Venture Global LNG](https://lailluminator.com/2025/09/25/cameron-parish-fishermen-clash-with-venture-global-over-dredging-mess/ "https://lailluminator.com/2025/09/25/cameron-parish-fishermen-clash-with-venture-global-over-dredging-mess/"), at a gain of [1,211.8% in exempted taxes](https://www.energy.gov/documents/049-louisiana-legislative-auditor-industrial-tax-exemption-programpdf "https://www.energy.gov/documents/049-louisiana-legislative-auditor-industrial-tax-exemption-programpdf"), to the corporation of course.
